One-line: renders one of the 25 official Enactus icons (line art on the painted gold circle) at a chosen size, with an optional caption.

```jsx
<BrandIcon name="handshake" size="lg" label="Partnership" basePath="../.." />
```

Use these for value/pillar rows, agenda markers and stat callouts. Do not substitute a flat, filled or multi-colour icon set, and do not hand-draw replacements — if the concept you need isn't in `BRAND_ICONS`, request a new icon from social@enactus.ca.
