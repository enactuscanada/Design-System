National Exposition Type sits on the protection gradient
Crop — hard rectangles, square corners, no border, no rounding. Full-bleed wherever the layout allows.
Colour — warm, bright, full colour, in-camera. Never duotone, desaturated, black-and-white or grain-filtered.
Type over photo — use --overlay-protect-dark, a bottom-up scrim. Never a translucent capsule or a blurred plate.
Never place the logo over a busy area of the image, or tint a photo gold.
