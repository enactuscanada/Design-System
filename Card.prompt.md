One-line: the standard content container — use it for project cards, stat blocks and any grouped content.

```jsx
<Card accent interactive>
  <h3>Team of the Year</h3>
  <p>Enactus Memorial took the national title in 2025.</p>
</Card>
```

Tones: `default` (white paper on off-white page), `sunken`, `brand` (gold fill — black text only), `inverse` (Almost Black), `outline`. `accent` adds the 3px gold top rule that stands in for the brand's folded-edge motif. Radius stays at 6px; do not round further or add coloured shadows.
