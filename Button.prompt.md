One-line: the standard Enactus action control — use `primary` for the single main action on a screen or slide, `secondary` for the alternative.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Apply to a team</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Learn more</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (gold fill, black label), `secondary` (2px black outline that inverts to solid black on hover), `ghost` (bare label), `inverse` (off-white fill for dark or photographic backgrounds), `danger`. Hover darkens rather than lightens; press shrinks to 98.5%. Never put white text on Enactus Gold.

Matches the buttons on Enactus Cactus: gold fill, black Medium label in sentence-style Title Case ("View Training Sessions", "Update My Profile"), 6px corners, a soft lift shadow. Labels say exactly what happens next — "View …", "Update …".
