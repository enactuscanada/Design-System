## Rules taken from the toolkit

**Copy:** follow CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS in the root readme: name the person, school and result; describe the project in plain words; use sourced numbers (deck defaults are from the 2024–25 annual report); use real, attributed student quotes.

- **Grounds:** mostly white. Colour comes from flat panels in Enactus Gold, Almost Black, or one SDG colour (green, pink, blue, crimson). Gradient images are for social and event graphics, not body slides.
- **Titles:** Knockout, title case (all caps on the cover only). Body is Roboto.
- **Logo:** the full logo on the cover and quote slides only, at 72px or larger at 1280×720 (3/4in minimum). Every other slide carries the gold origami mark alone, top-right — never directly under or beside the full logo.
- **Edge bar:** white slides carry a gold / black / gold bar on the left edge.
- **Photos:** real Enactus photos — pull more from the Enactus Canada Flickr (flickr.com/photos/enactus_canada). Never crop through a face: set `object-position` so every face stays fully in frame, and pick a photo whose people fit the panel shape rather than forcing a wide group into a narrow panel. Circles only for single portraits.
- **Never repeat a person within a deck.** Across the deck, show a mix of genders, races, schools and regions (the Flickr has albums for every regional and the national exposition), and include faculty and judges, not only students.
- **No** eyebrow labels, decorative rules under titles, or footer text.
- White text only on SDG colours dark enough for it: green #3F7E44, pink #A8104F, blue #00689D, crimson #A21942. The lighter SDG colours (yellow, orange, sky) hold black text or act as accents.
